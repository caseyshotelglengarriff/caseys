Welcome

A village hotel with a long history

Dating back to the 1880s, Casey's is a classic ivy-covered hotel just a 3-minute walk from the Glengarriff Nature Reserve, 1.2 km from Bamboo Park and 2.5 km from Glengarriff Golf Club.

In 1884 the hotel opened its door to the weary traveller, and that door has been open ever since under the proud care of the same family. It has always been a place of relaxation, with a personal, friendly service and old-fashioned courtesy, and is today a focal point for visitors from all over the world.

Glengarriff is a place to enjoy in any season. In winter the village is quiet and peaceful, and indoor pleasures are warm and cosy with a drink by the fireside. On a calm evening the water, like glass, reflects the boats and the shoreline.

Explore

Things to do

Glengarriff and the West Cork area is full of wonderful things to do for all interests, ages and budgets. Here are some ideas to help plan your stay.

The Beara Peninsula

Days of enjoyment.

  • Drive the Ring of Beara from Glengarriff towards Dursey and Kenmare
  • Ride Ireland's only cable car to Dursey Island
  • Visit the Allihies Copper Mine Museum
  • Take the ferry to Bere Island for its forts, walks and heritage centre
  • Walk or cycle a section of the Beara Way
More info

Mizen Peninsula

A day trip from our hotel.

  • Visit the signal station at Mizen Head, Ireland's most south-westerly point
  • Cross the arched footbridge and watch for whales, dolphins and seabirds
  • Explore the keepers' quarters, engine room and Marconi radio room
  • Swim at nearby Barleycove Beach
  • See the Altar Wedge Tomb
More info

Glengarriff

Ireland's garden heaven.

  • Walk the trails in Glengarriff Woods Nature Reserve, free and open daily
  • Follow the 3.1 km Esknamucky Walk through the woods
  • Visit the Bamboo Park, with 30 kinds of bamboo and harbour views
  • Stroll to the Blue Pool, a tidal inlet beside the village
  • Take a boat to Garinish Island
More info

Healy's Pass

A spectacular drive.

  • Drive the 12 km of hairpin bends between Adrigole and Lauragh
  • Look out over Glanmore Lake and Kenmare Bay
  • Cycle over the pass on the Beara Way Cycle Route
More info

Sheep's Head

Walks, trails & fun.

  • Walk the Sheep's Head Way, or one of the shorter looped trails
  • Follow the Lighthouse Loop to the lighthouse at the tip
  • Look out for the Napoleonic signal tower, stone circles and old churches
  • Cycle the peninsula on the Sheep's Head cycling route
  • Watch for dolphins and whales off the headland
More info

Garinish Island

Stunning gardens.

  • Take the short ferry or waterbus from Glengarriff Harbour
  • Watch for seals, and perhaps sea eagles, on the crossing
  • Wander the Italian and walled gardens
  • See the Grecian Temple overlooking the sea and the Caha Mountains
  • Visit Bryce House (guided tours are limited in the shoulder season)
More info

Gougane Barra

Walking trails.

  • Choose one of six walking trails, from a short nature path to Slí na Sléibhe in the mountains
  • Visit St Finbarr's Oratory on the lake island
  • Walk beside the River Lee, which rises in these hills
  • Take the 3 km scenic drive through the forest park
More info

On the water

Things to do on the water.

  • Kayak the sheltered coves of Glengarriff Bay from the old fishing pier
  • Take the ferry to Garinish Island past the seal colony
  • Visit the Blue Pool, a tidal inlet in the woods
  • Look out for sea eagles on the harbour crossing
More info
