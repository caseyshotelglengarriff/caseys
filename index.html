Welcome

A village hotel with a long history

Dating back to the 1880s, Casey's is a classic ivy-covered hotel just a 3-minute walk from the Glengarriff Nature Reserve, 1.2 km from Bamboo Park and 2.5 km from Glengarriff Golf Club.
