Casey's Hotel

3-star hotel · The Village, Glengarriff, Co. Cork · Rated 4.5 on Google (525 reviews)

Ivy-clad hotel with classic rooms, an Irish restaurant and a cosy bar with live music and a patio.

Dating back to the 1880s, this classic ivy-covered hotel is a 3-minute walk from the Glengarriff Nature Reserve and 1.2 km from Bamboo Park and 2.5 km from the Glengarriff Golf Club.
